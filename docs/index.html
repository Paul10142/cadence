Thyme Custom

A tiny stopwatch, countdown and pomodoro timer that lives in your Mac's menu bar. No dock icon, no window, no account.

Download for macOS

Free and open source · Apple Silicon · macOS 13 or later

What it does

Stopwatch

Click the icon and it starts counting up. Click again to pause. Finish saves the time to your history and resets.

Countdown

Set a duration and choose what happens at zero — blink in the menu bar, an alert window, a notification, or a spoken announcement.

Pomodoro

Work and break lengths over a set number of rounds, cycling automatically. A rule under the time means you're working; a rule above it means you're on a break.

History

Every session is listed with a marker showing which timer produced it, a running total, and a CSV export.

Installing

  1. Download the DMG, open it, and drag Thyme Custom into Applications.
  2. Open it. A stopwatch appears in your menu bar — there's no window and no dock icon, which is the whole idea.

If macOS says it's damaged

It isn't. This app isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer certificate, and macOS is wary of anything downloaded without one. Paste this into Terminal once: 

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Thyme Custom.app"

That clears the "downloaded from the internet" flag. Then open it normally. Alternatively, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

Only do this for software you trust. The complete source is on GitHub, and you can build it yourself instead.

Where it came from

Thyme by João Moreno was a lovely menu bar stopwatch, last updated in 2015 and no longer able to run on Apple Silicon Macs. This is an independent rewrite in Swift with a countdown and pomodoro added. It imports your old Thyme history automatically.
